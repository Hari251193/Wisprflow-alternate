# Daily Report Methodology (followed on every run)

## 1. Market context (today)
- Nifty 50 / Sensex / Nifty Midcap 150 close & % change; India VIX.
- FII/DII net cash flows (NSE provisional data).
- Key macro: RBI policy, CPI/IIP/GDP, crude, USD/INR, US yields, global cues.
- Sector leaders/laggards of the day.
- Nifty 50 P/E vs historical range → overall stance (Deploy fully / Stagger / Wait).

## 2. Stock screening filters (large & quality mid caps, mcap > ₹20,000 cr)
Fundamental quality (must pass most):
- ROE and ROCE > 15% (3-yr avg); improving or stable.
- Revenue & profit CAGR > 12% (3–5 yr); latest quarter not deteriorating.
- Debt/Equity < 0.5 (ex-banks/NBFCs; for financials use GNPA/NNPA, CAR, ROA).
- Positive & growing operating cash flow; CFO/PAT > 0.8.
- Reasonable valuation: P/E or P/B vs own 5-yr median and peers; PEG < 2.
Governance & ownership:
- Promoter holding stable/rising; promoter pledge < 5% (flag any increase).
- FII + DII/MF holding trend over last 2–4 quarters (accumulation = positive).
- No auditor resignations, SEBI actions, related-party red flags.
Catalysts & news:
- Recent results, guidance, order wins, capex, policy tailwinds (PLI, defence, infra, etc.).
- Block/bulk deals, insider buying, buybacks, rating changes.
Technical entry check (timing only, not selection):
- Price vs 50/200 DMA; RSI(14) not > 75 (overheated); distance from 52-wk high.
- Give a suggested accumulation range and a "do not chase above" level.

## 3. Output per pick
Name & NSE symbol · CMP · suggested buy range · conviction (High/Med) · 3–5 bullet thesis ·
key metrics (ROE, ROCE, D/E, P/E vs median, promoter %, pledge, FII/DII trend) · risks ·
review trigger (what would make us exit). Cite sources.

## 4. Allocation suggestion
Given ₹30–50k/month, suggest how today's/this month's stock money could be split
(max ~20% of stock bucket per name, 8–15 names over time, sector cap ~30%).

## 5. Weekly (Friday report): MF / bond corner
Core-portfolio suggestions: index/flexi-cap/large-mid funds, debt (liquid, short duration,
G-sec/target-maturity, RBI bonds/SGB-type options), with expense ratio & rolling returns.

## 6. Scorecard
Maintain `picks.csv`: every new pick is appended (date, symbol, price at pick, conviction, thesis).
Each report shows performance since pick vs Nifty 50 over the same period, and flags
any pick whose thesis is broken (downgrade / exit view).

## Rules
- Never fabricate numbers. If a figure can't be verified, say "unverified" or omit.
- Prefer quality over quantity: 0–3 new picks per day is fine; "no new buy today" is a valid answer.
- Always include the disclaimer: research only, not SEBI-registered advice.
