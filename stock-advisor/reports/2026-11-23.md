## 2. New pick

### ③ Polycab India (NSE: POLYCAB) — MEDIUM conviction
- **Reference price:** ₹7,570 (20 Nov close; the 23 Nov close is unverified, and midcaps fell ~1% today). 52-wk high ₹10,126, so ~25% below it.
- **Buy range:** ₹7,300–7,650 · **Don't chase above ₹7,900**
- **Thesis:**
  - India's largest wires & cables maker. Q2 FY27: revenue +53% (₹9,899 cr), profit +43% (₹990 cr), both records.
  - The FMEG (fans, lights, switches) division turned around, with EBIT margin up from 0.5% to 10.1% in a year.
  - It benefits from power-grid capex, data centres, real estate and exports.
  - **Red flag cleared:** on 30 Oct 2026, NCLAT set aside the NCLT order that had started IBC proceedings, and NSE withdrew the F&O exclusion.
  - Valuation: P/E ~36. On ~43% profit growth that's a PEG under 1. CLSA has Outperform with a ₹10,240 target.
- **Ownership:** promoters ~61.4% (stable). FIIs ~18.1% (Sep 2026, down from 19.0% QoQ, though up a lot YoY). Pledge: unverified.
- **Risks:**
  - High copper prices: JM Financial expects weaker revenue growth over the next 12 months.
  - UltraTech (Aditya Birla) entering wires and cables.
  - EBITDA margin is down YoY (14.4% vs 15.8%).
  - The IBC episode could resurface if creditors appeal further.
- **Exit/review trigger:** EBITDA margin below 12% for 2 quarters, any new insolvency or governance action, or cable revenue growth falling below 15%.
