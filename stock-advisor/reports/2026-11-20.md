## 2. New picks

### ① HDFC Bank (NSE: HDFCBANK) — HIGH conviction
- **CMP:** ₹821.80 (19 Nov close) · 52-wk range ₹669–₹1,017 (~19% below the high)
- **Buy range:** ₹800–835 · **Don't chase above ₹860**
- **Thesis:**
  - India's highest-quality private bank, at a multi-year-low valuation: P/E ~16, P/B ~2.1 (sources vary). The shares are down ~28% this year.
  - Q2 FY27: deposits +18.8%, loans +16.3% YoY. Excluding treasury, profit grew ~12.6%, though headline profit was only +4.7% because of a ₹1,610 cr treasury drop.
  - Asset quality improving: GNPA 1.11% (vs 1.24% a year ago), NNPA 0.37%, credit cost 0.44%.
  - The CEO overhang has cleared. Anup Bagchi took charge on 27 Oct, with RBI approval for 3 years.
- **Ownership:** no promoter (widely held). FII/DII trend: unverified this run.
- **Risks:** NIM compression (3.26% → 3.16% QoQ). New-CEO execution risk (Moody's flagged it). Further RBI hikes could raise deposit costs.
- **Exit/review trigger:** NIM below 3.0% for 2 straight quarters, GNPA above 1.5%, or deposit growth below 12%.

### ② HCL Technologies (NSE: HCLTECH) — MEDIUM conviction
- **CMP:** ~₹1,345 (18 Nov close; IT fell ~1.1% on 19 Nov, so slightly lower likely) · 52-wk range ₹1,030–₹1,780 (~24% below the high)
- **Buy range:** ₹1,300–1,360 (near the 200-DMA ~₹1,340) · **Don't chase above ₹1,420**
- **Thesis:**
  - Q2 FY27 was the best of the large-cap IT pack. Revenue was ₹35,620 cr (+11.5% YoY), net profit ₹5,132 cr (+21.2%), a record quarter.
  - FY27 constant-currency guidance was raised to 2.5–4.0%. EBIT margin rose 131 bps QoQ to 18.2%.
  - AI revenue is $219M (+78% YoY), about an $876M run rate. Deal bookings were $3.3B (+27% YoY).
  - Anand Rathi upgraded the stock to BUY. P/E is ~20–27 (sources vary), which is reasonable versus its history.
- **Risks:** a US tech/AI selloff (Nasdaq −2.2% overnight), weaker software revenue, softer H1 operating cash flow, and rupee moves.
- **Exit/review trigger:** a guidance cut, EBIT margin below 17%, or 2 straight quarters of falling bookings.

### ❌ Rejected: Polycab (POLYCAB)
Q2 profit rose 43% and revenue 53%, and the stock is ~25% off its high at a P/E ~36. However, reports from October say NSE will drop it from F&O from 30 Dec and mention an IBC-related shortlisting. Competition from UltraTech and high copper costs add pressure. **I won't recommend it until the IBC/F&O news is clarified.** It stays on the watchlist.
